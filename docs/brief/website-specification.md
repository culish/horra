# PT. Horra Prime Group — Website Specification

## 1. Project Overview

Build a polished, responsive corporate website for **PT. Horra Prime Group**, presenting the company as a multi-service Indonesian business partner for international clients, investors, traders, and travelers.

The website should communicate trust, professionalism, operational clarity, and international readiness. It should feel suitable for a large Indonesian company: confident visual hierarchy, restrained motion, excellent typography, consistent spacing, strong photography, and clear conversion paths.

1. **Business Consulting**

2. **International Trading**

   - Coal & Minerals
   - Seafood & Marine Products
   - Minerals
3. **Tours & Travel**
   - Ticket & Hotel
   - Tour Package

   - Travel Guide

Use the supplied PDF as the content source. Some listed divisions have limited detail in the PDF; do not invent specifications, certifications, export volumes, client logos, awards, office addresses, or testimonials. Use clearly marked placeholders for information that the company must supply.

## 2. Website Objectives

- Establish a credible corporate digital presence.
- Explain the three business divisions and their services.
- Generate qualified inquiries from prospective clients.
- Make it easy to ask about visa and immigration assistance.
- Present trading product categories to international buyers.
- Help travelers discover ticket, hotel, and tour assistance.
- Work well on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Support future growth with reusable components and a scalable page structure.

## 3. Target Audiences

- Foreign nationals planning a visit to Indonesia.
- Investors and entrepreneurs exploring company establishment in Indonesia.
- Businesses looking for Indonesian commodity suppliers.
- Travelers seeking ticket, hotel, transfer, or tour assistance.
- Indonesian and international corporate partners.

## 6. C1 Tourist Visa Page Content

Use the supplied company profile as the source for the initial draft.

### Page title
**C1 Tourist Visa for Indonesia**

### Introduction
The company profile describes the C1 Tourist Visa as a single-entry visit visa for permitted purposes such as tourism, visiting family or friends, attending exhibitions, and non-employment business visits. The profile states an initial stay validity of up to 60 days and notes that extensions may be possible under Indonesian immigration rules.

**Important:** This content must be checked against current official Indonesian immigration guidance before publication. Do not present this page as legal advice or guarantee eligibility, approval, duration, extension, or conversion.

### Service process listed in the profile
1. Free consultation
2. Eligibility assessment
3. Document verification
4. Visa application submission
5. Immigration processing
6. E-visa approval
7. Entry to Indonesia
8. Visa extension assistance
9. Investor KITAS conversion assistance, where eligible and permitted

### Documents listed in the profile
- Passport with at least six months of validity
- Passport-size photo
- Flight itinerary
- Hotel booking or accommodation details
- Bank statement for the last six months
- Additional supporting documents depending on nationality

Present these as a preliminary checklist only. Clearly state that exact requirements vary by applicant and must be verified before applying.

### Who may need a visa?
The supplied profile says foreign nationals who are not eligible for visa-free entry or Visa on Arrival/e-VOA may need to obtain a visa before travel. Do not hard-code the profile's nationality list as a current legal determination. Instead, direct visitors to check current official eligibility rules or request an assessment.

### C1 Tourist Visa to Investor KITAS
The profile says conversion may be possible after establishing a PT PMA and meeting immigration requirements. Present this only as conditional, subject to current law, eligibility, and approval by authorities. Do not imply that every C1 visa holder can convert.

### Why contact Horra Prime Group?
- Professional immigration consultation
- Visa extension assistance
- Business setup support
- Investor KITAS assistance
- End-to-end coordination
- Customer support

## 10. Visual Design System

### Overall direction
Premium Indonesian corporate, modern international B2B, editorial, confident, minimal, warm, and trustworthy.

### Suggested palette
- Deep navy: `#10243A`
- Forest green: `#176B55`
- Warm ivory: `#F7F5F0`
- Charcoal: `#222B35`
- Muted gray: `#667085`
- Accent gold: `#C6A56B`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Use navy and ivory as the primary foundation. Green can signal Indonesian nature and trade; gold should be a restrained accent rather than a dominant color.

### Typography
Use a clean contemporary sans-serif such as **Inter**, **Manrope**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Keep headings strong and concise, body copy readable, and line lengths comfortable.

### Components
- Responsive header and mega-menu
- Hero section
- Division cards
- Service cards
- Product category cards
- Breadcrumbs
- Process steps
- CTA banners
- Inquiry forms
- FAQ accordion
- Testimonials only when real approved testimonials are provided
- Footer
- Toast or inline form submission states

### Motion
Subtle entrance transitions, gentle hover states, and restrained scroll effects. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not make the site feel like a flashy startup template.

## 11. Functional Requirements

- Responsive layout at mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop breakpoints.
- Functional internal navigation and meaningful URLs.
- Working mobile menu and dropdowns.
- Form validation with clear error messages.
- Accessible success and failure states.
- WhatsApp CTA only after the company confirms the official number; use a placeholder until then.
- Email and telephone links only when verified details are provided.
- Search-engine-friendly page titles, descriptions, headings, and semantic HTML.
- Optimized images and lazy loading below the fold.
- Accessible keyboard navigation, visible focus states, alt text, and adequate contrast.
- Privacy-conscious collection of inquiry information.
- No fabricated company details, customer logos, certifications, statistics, or testimonials.

## 12. Recommended Technical Stack

Preferred option for a maintainable custom build:
- Next.js with TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Reusable React components
- Lucide icons
- A form service or backend endpoint added only when credentials and requirements are known

If the existing project uses another framework, preserve it rather than replacing the stack unnecessarily. Do not claim that forms send messages unless an actual backend or service is configured.
